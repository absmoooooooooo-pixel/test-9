Weight Calculation Formula

W = [(√3 ÷ 2) × A² − (π ÷ 4) × d²] × L × ρ ÷ 10⁶

W = Weight (kg) · A = Across-flats of hexagon (mm) · d = Hole diameter (mm) · L = Length (mm) · ρ = Density (g/cm³)

How to Calculate (Step-by-Step)

Carbon Steel Hex Bar with Round Hole
  1. Convert A, d, and L to millimetres (mm) if entered in inches.
  2. Calculate outer hexagon area: Ahex = (√3/2) × A² (mm²).
  3. Calculate round hole area: Ahole = (π/4) × d² (mm²).
  4. Compute net area = Ahex − Ahole, then weight W = net area × L × ρ ÷ 1,000,000 (kg).
  5. Multiply by quantity for total weight.

Variables: A is the outer regular hexagon across-flats dimension, d is the concentric round hole diameter, L is length, and ρ is density. Hole must fit inside the hexagon.

Assumptions/limitations: Assumes a regular hexagonal bar with a concentric circular through-hole and uniform dimensions. Actual weight may vary due to tolerances and material grade.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate the weight of a hex bar with a round hole?

Take the solid hexagon area (√3 ÷ 2) × A², subtract the bore (π ÷ 4) × d², then multiply by length and density. The result is the steel between the flats and the hole.

What does a 40 mm across-flats hex bar with a 20 mm hole weigh per metre?

The hexagon is 1,385.6 mm² and the 20 mm bore removes 314.2 mm², leaving 1,071.4 mm², which is about 8.41 kg/m. The same bar without the hole weighs 10.88 kg/m.

How large can the hole be for a given across-flats size?

The bore has to stay inside the flats, so the hole diameter must be smaller than A; in practice the wall left around the hole must still carry the load. The calculator accepts any positive d, so check your drawing for the minimum wall thickness.

What is this profile used for?

Bored hexagon bar is stock for socket-type components: lock nuts, coupling nuts, hex bushes, valve parts and machined fasteners that need a hex outside and a bore inside.

Does the calculator assume the hole runs the full length?

Yes. It treats the bore as a through hole of constant diameter, so a blind or stepped bore will weigh more than the calculated figure.

How much does changing the hole from 10 mm to 30 mm save?

On a 40 mm across-flats bar the bore removes 0.62 kg/m at 10 mm, 2.47 kg/m at 20 mm and 5.55 kg/m at 30 mm, taking the bar from 10.88 kg/m down to 5.33 kg/m at the largest size.

Can I enter the bore in inches?

Yes, switch the unit to Inches and give the across-flats, hole diameter and length in inches. The output remains in kilograms because the density list is metric.

Why is the calculated weight different from the cut piece on the scale?

The hexagon corners are rounded, the bore may be slightly oversize after drilling or broaching, and bar is rolled to a minus tolerance. All three make the real piece a little lighter than the sharp-corner theory.
