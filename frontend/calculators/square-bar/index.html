Weight Calculation Formula

W = A × B × L × ρ ÷ 10⁶

W = Weight (kg) · A = Width (mm) · B = Height (mm) · L = Length (mm) · ρ = Density (g/cm³)

How to Calculate (Step-by-Step)

Carbon Steel Square Bar
  1. Convert all dimensions to millimetres (mm) if entered in inches (1 in = 25.4 mm).
  2. Calculate cross-sectional area: Area = A × B (mm²).
  3. Calculate volume: V = A × B × L (mm³).
  4. Calculate weight: W = V × ρ ÷ 1,000,000 (kg), where ρ is in g/cm³.
  5. Multiply by quantity for total weight.

Variables: A is the width and B is the height of the solid cross-section, L is length, and ρ is material density.

Assumptions/limitations: Assumes a solid, straight square or rectangular bar with uniform cross-section and no holes. Actual weight may vary due to tolerances, corner radii, and material grade.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate the weight of a square bar?

Multiply the two sides to get the cross-section area, then multiply by length and density. The full formula is A × B × L × ρ ÷ 10⁶ for millimetres and g/cm³.

What is the weight of a 20 mm square bar per metre?

About 3.14 kg/m in mild steel, since the area is 400 mm². The shop shortcut is A² ÷ 127, which gives the same result for steel.

What does a 25 mm square bar weigh?

4.91 kg per metre and roughly 29.4 kg for a 6 m length. Square bar is stocked in 6 m lengths, so the piece weight is the number used for lifting and transport.

Can I use this calculator for a rectangular or flat bar?

Yes. Enter the width as A and the thickness as B; a 100 × 50 mm bar has an area of 5,000 mm² and weighs about 39.25 kg/m. Square is simply the case where A equals B.

Why is a square bar heavier than a round bar of the same nominal size?

A square of side D fills 4 ÷ π times the area of a circle of diameter D. A 25 mm square bar weighs 4.91 kg/m while a 25 mm round bar weighs 3.85 kg/m.

How do I work out the weight of a bar from its length in metres?

Use A × B × 0.00785 kg per metre with A and B in millimetres, then multiply by the length in metres. For a 20 mm bar 3 m long: 400 × 0.00785 × 3 = 9.42 kg.

Does stainless steel square bar weigh more than mild steel?

Yes, slightly. At 8.00 g/cm³ stainless steel is about 1.9% heavier than the 7.85 g/cm³ mild-steel result for the same dimensions.

How do I enter dimensions in inches?

Select Inches and type the two sides and the length in inches. The output remains in kilograms; if you prefer to work by hand, multiply each inch dimension by 25.4 mm first.

Why is the mill weight a little under the calculated value?

Rolled square bar has rounded corners and is produced to a minus size tolerance, so the delivered bar contains slightly less steel than the sharp-cornered nominal section.
