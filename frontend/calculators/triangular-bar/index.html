Weight Calculation Formula

W = (B × H ÷ 2) × L × ρ ÷ 10⁶

W = Weight (kg) · B = Base (mm) · H = Perpendicular height (mm) · L = Length (mm) · ρ = Density (g/cm³)

How to Calculate (Step-by-Step)

Carbon Steel Triangular Bar
  1. Convert B, H, and L to millimetres (mm) if entered in inches.
  2. Calculate triangular cross-sectional area: Area = (B × H) ÷ 2 (mm²).
  3. Calculate volume: V = Area × L (mm³).
  4. Calculate weight: W = V × ρ ÷ 1,000,000 (kg).
  5. Multiply by quantity for total weight.

Variables: B is the triangle base, H is the perpendicular height, L is bar length, and ρ is material density.

Assumptions/limitations: Assumes a solid uniform triangular bar and H is the perpendicular height to base B. Actual weight may vary with tolerances and material grade.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate the weight of a triangular bar?

The area of a triangle is half the base times the perpendicular height, (B × H) ÷ 2. Multiply that by the bar length and the density to get the weight.

What is the weight of a 50 mm base, 40 mm high triangular bar per metre?

The area is 50 × 40 ÷ 2 = 1,000 mm², so one metre weighs about 7.85 kg in mild steel. A 3 m piece is roughly 23.6 kg.

Does the triangle have to be a right triangle?

No. Any triangle with base B and perpendicular height H has the same area, so scalene and isosceles triangular bars are calculated correctly as long as you enter the true perpendicular height.

How do I find the height of an equilateral triangular bar?

For an equilateral triangle H = 0.866 × side. A 40 mm equilateral section has a height of 34.64 mm and an area of 692.8 mm², about 5.44 kg/m.

How much lighter is a triangular bar than a rectangular bar of the same size?

Exactly half, because the triangle is half of the rectangle with the same base and height. A 50 × 40 triangle (7.85 kg/m) is half of a 50 × 40 flat bar (15.70 kg/m).

What are triangular bars used for?

Machine keys, guide rails, wear strips, corner fillets and decorative or structural profiles. The weight per metre is what matters when ordering mill lengths.

Can I enter the dimensions in inches?

Yes. Choose Inches, enter the base, height and length in inches, and the result is still in kilograms.

How does bar length change the result?

Weight is directly proportional to length: doubling the length doubles the mass. Enter the cut length if you are weighing pieces rather than ordering stock bars.

Why is the actual bar weight slightly different?

Rolled triangular bar has rounded corners and is produced within dimensional tolerances, so the sharp-corner calculation is a nominal value rather than a guaranteed mass.
