Weight Calculation Formula

W = [H × Tw + 2 × (B − Tw) × Tf] × L × ρ ÷ 10⁶

W = Weight (kg) · B = Flange width (mm) · H = Overall height (mm) · Tf = Flange thickness (mm) · Tw = Web thickness (mm) · L = Length (mm) · ρ = Density (g/cm³)

How to Calculate (Step-by-Step)

Carbon Steel C Channel
  1. Convert B, H, Tf, Tw, and L to millimetres (mm) if entered in inches.
  2. Calculate cross-sectional area: Area = H×Tw + 2×(B − Tw)×Tf (mm²).
  3. Calculate volume: V = Area × L (mm³).
  4. Calculate weight: W = V × ρ ÷ 1,000,000 (kg).
  5. Multiply by quantity for total weight.

Variables: B is flange width, H overall channel height, Tf flange thickness, Tw web thickness, L length, ρ density. Assumes web runs full height and flanges project from web.

Assumptions/limitations: Theoretical C-channel geometry with sharp corners and uniform thicknesses; web taken through full height. Actual rolled channels may differ due to radii, tapers, and standard tolerances.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate the weight of a C channel?

Treat the channel as one web plate plus two flange plates. This calculator uses H × Tw + 2 × (B − Tw) × Tf for the area, then multiplies by length and density, so the web/flange overlap is counted only once.

What is the weight of a 100 × 50 mm C channel with a 5 mm web and 8 mm flange per metre?

The area is 100 × 5 + 2 × 45 × 8 = 1,220 mm², which is about 9.58 kg/m in mild steel. A 6 m length weighs roughly 57.5 kg.

Why is the flange length written as (B − Tw)?

The flange width B is measured across the complete channel, so the part already included in the web has to be removed. Using (B − Tw) for each flange avoids counting the web twice.

Will this match the ISMC or IS 2062 catalogue weight?

Not exactly. Published channel tables include the root radius where the flange meets the web and, for many rolled channels, a small flange taper; both add a little steel. This calculator uses a sharp-corner, constant-thickness model, so catalogue values are usually slightly higher.

Are tapered flanges included in the calculation?

No. The flange is treated as a constant-thickness plate of Tf across its full width. If your channel has a measurable taper, use the average flange thickness for a closer estimate.

What is the difference between a C channel and a U channel?

They describe the same open section; the letter only reflects how the profile is drawn or which standard the mill follows. Enter B, H, flange thickness and web thickness and the weight calculation is identical.

How much does a 3 m length of 100 × 50 C channel weigh?

At 9.58 kg/m a 3 m piece is about 28.7 kg. Enter 3000 mm as the length, or keep the length at 1 m and multiply the per-metre figure yourself.

Can I calculate a stainless steel or aluminium channel here?

Yes. Choose the material from the list or enter a custom density. Only the density changes, so a stainless channel of the same dimensions is about 1.9% heavier than mild steel and an aluminium one about 66% lighter.

Why does a taller channel weigh more at the same thickness?

The web area is H × Tw, so every extra millimetre of height adds steel along the full length. Flange width and flange thickness add weight more slowly because the flanges are only two narrow strips.
